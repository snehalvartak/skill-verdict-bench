Where it fails

Share of skills flagged, by source and label, at the current threshold. Green outline: behaving (at least 70% of malicious flagged, or at most 30% of benign). Orange: not.

Why interactiveA pattern here is a claim about specific skills. Clicking a cell takes you to them in the explorer below.

SkillHarm. Most of its malicious skills score low on all five questions. A reading of 20 sampled misses found 18 where SKILL.md only tells the agent to complete a mandatory preflight or setup step in another file that is not in the dataset, so there is no payload text for the questions to see. SkillScreen also reads SKILL.md only, so its high SkillHarm recall is not from reading those files; it may come from learning that phrasing or the source's style, which this data cannot separate from detecting harm. The sample was chosen by score, and the benchmark label, not the text, says these skills are harmful.

Does the validation threshold transfer?

Each threshold was chosen to give 5% false positives on validation. This is the rate it produced on the test sources, with 95% intervals. It follows the test-set switch and is fixed by the pre-registration.
